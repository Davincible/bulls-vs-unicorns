🐂 ⚔ 🦄 Welcome to the Arena

Two token armies fight in 60-second rounds. Here's the loop:

1. Get funds — open the 👛 wallet (top right), connect, grab test tokens, deposit.
2. Deploy — pick an amount and toggle 🐂 and/or 🦄. Your fighter enters the next round.
3. Fight — collisions raid coins off the enemy. Normal: winnings compound in the ring (win big, lose big). Extraction: raids bank straight to your balance (safer).
4. Withdraw — cash your balance back to your wallet any time.

Every round is provably fair — the ✓ pill up top lets you re-verify any result yourself. House fee is 0.2% on deploys, and only the amount both sides can match is ever at risk (the excess is refunded).
