### The gap Sat Rush exposes, which is a product note rather than a copy note

Their tweets 4-6 are all one thing: **a reason to come back tomorrow.** The Stack accrues, the release
fee pays holders who stay, Hash Rate Points accumulate toward Vault entries. Three separate mechanics
whose only job is to make round 2 matter more than round 1.

Bulls ⚔ Unicorns has no equivalent. A round ends, it settles, and you are exactly where you started.
Every mechanic we have is *within* one round — EXTRACT is a decision about the next thirty seconds,
not about next week. That is why our thread has to lean on proof (tweet 9) where theirs leans on
progression: we do not currently have a progression to describe.

Worth deciding before a real launch push, because it is the difference between a thread that converts
into players and one that converts into spectators. The deck's "distributions back to the communities"
is the closest thing and could become that hook if it were tied to participation.
